GOOD TO KNOW

Before you arrive,
let’s answer your questions.

We want you to focus on enjoying the trip. For more details, reach our team directly on WhatsApp.

Talk to us
How can I book a cabin?

Enter your travel dates, full name and contact phone number. WhatsApp will open with a message for you to send to the hotel, where rates and availability will be confirmed.

How much does it cost to travel from Guatemala City to Huehuetenango?

Reference fares shared by the hotel: express bus Q145 one way or Q285 round trip; light aircraft Q995 one way or Q1,800 round trip; and Uber Q3,200 with trip details to be confirmed. Fares may change, and the last leg to Chiantla may be additional. See transport options.

What are your contact hours?

Our contact hours are 8:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Please confirm actual check-in and check-out times with the hotel.

Do you have accommodations for families and couples?

Yes. We offer alpine cabins, family cabins and rooms for couples. Please confirm each option’s features and occupancy before booking.

How can I find out about rates?

Rates and availability are confirmed directly by the hotel. Tell us your dates, number of guests and preferred lodging type.

Do seasonal offers have an expiry date?

Yes, offers change by date. Ask us on WhatsApp for valid rates and conditions.

Can I book dinner or a BBQ?

Please ask the hotel about current package inclusions, rates and conditions.
